01

Rebuilding ICP, GTM & Funnel Quality
for Enterprise Laundry Growth

Quick Clean serves hospitality and healthcare with on-premise commercial laundry solutions. The priority was to sharpen ICP, structure GTM, and build a multi-channel engine for high-value enterprise pipeline.

Growth Challenge

  • Strong operations, but ICP and marketing motion needed sharper definition
  • Enterprise accounts had long cycles and multiple decision-makers
  • Messaging needed to differ across greenfield hotels, brownfield hotels, greenfield hospitals, and brownfield hospitals
  • Each segment had different pain points, objections, and buying triggers

Strategy

  • Redefined ICP using property type, stage, size, laundry volume, current setup, cost pressure, hygiene needs, and readiness to shift
  • Prioritized hotels with 150+ rooms and hospitals with 250+ beds/rooms where laundry volume justified a managed model
  • Built 4 core segments: greenfield hotels, brownfield hotels, greenfield hospitals, and brownfield hospitals
  • Created different messaging for zero-capex setup, pay-per-kg model, operational reliability, sustainability, and infection-control workflows
  • Built a multi-channel engine across email, calling, LinkedIn, website, WhatsApp, and sales enablement
  • Used industry events like Hotelier India, Global Design & Development Confluence, Hotelier India Awards, and GM Summit to build visibility and direct access to decision-makers

Business Impact

  • Improved MQL to SQL conversion by 40%
  • Created clearer segmentation and qualification journeys
  • Improved sales readiness through better messaging and collaterals
  • Improved CAC efficiency by aligning targeting, messaging, and channel strategy
  • Built stronger conversations with hotel owners, GMs, hospitality leaders, and healthcare stakeholders
  • Supported consistent high-value pipeline through multi-channel marketing

Business from Zero

  • Served 5 recurring B2B clients with consistent service quality

Business Impact
